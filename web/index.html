A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW
A small guide.
 A smoother update.

      
Which browsers work?
Use a browser with Web Bluetooth, such as Chrome or Edge on a supported computer, or Chrome on Android. Safari and Firefox don’t provide this connection. Browsers on iPhone or iPad, including Chrome, cannot update directly here. Bluetooth availability also depends on your operating system and adapter.
Browsers embedded inside apps may not expose Bluetooth. Copy this page’s link into a supported browser and choose your firmware there. In Chrome on Linux, open chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features, enable Experimental Web Platform features, then relaunch Chrome. See the Chrome Web Bluetooth guide.

      
My watch isn’t in the list.
This page connects to goPine’s in-app updater, available in BLE firmware 0.3.22 and later. Open Settings → Firmware Update and hold to connect. Disconnect your phone app; if needed, temporarily turn off the phone’s Bluetooth. Keep the watch nearby.
An InfiniTime recovery clock or an older goPine build needs a one-time transition or wired setup first.

      
What if the connection drops?
Leave this page open. It reconnects to the same watch and continues from the bytes the watch has saved. You can also pause and resume. If the watch shows an error, cancel there, then choose Start over here.
Refreshing or closing this page discards the browser’s transfer session. Cancel the old transfer on the watch before starting a new one.

      
Where does the firmware come from?
The page downloads and checks this project’s latest published GitHub release automatically. Bluetooth only connects after you choose Connect & update and select your watch. You can also choose a local ZIP, which stays in your browser and goes directly to your watch. The updater checks its structure and checksums; these are integrity checks, not a digital signature. Choose local files from a source you trust.
